Smoked Meat (Combined Smoking Method)

BASE
1 kg meat (brisket, ribs, pork shoulder, chicken)
BBQ Beef Rub
¼ cup wood chips (hickory, applewood, mesquite)

METHOD
Season meat with rub.
Rest several hours or overnight.
Preheat smoker to 107°C.
Add soaked wood chips.
Smoke 3–6 hours depending on cut.
Optional glaze with BBQ sauce final stage.
Rest 10–15 minutes before slicing.
