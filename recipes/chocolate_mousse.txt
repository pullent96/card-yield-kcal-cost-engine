Simple Chocolate Mousse (Chef Version)

BASE
• 200 g dark chocolate
• 300 ml double cream
• 2 egg yolks (optional, richer)
• 2 egg whites
• 30 g sugar

METHOD
Melt chocolate gently over bain-marie, cool slightly.
Whip cream to soft peaks.
Whisk yolks into chocolate (optional).
Whip egg whites + sugar to soft peaks.
Fold cream into chocolate, then fold in whites.
Spoon into dishes, chill 2–3 hrs to set.

NOTES
Do not overmix — keep it light. Can set in moulds for cleaner plating.
