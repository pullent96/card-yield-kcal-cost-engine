Poached Egg — Universal Method (Chef Version)

BASE
• Fresh eggs
• Deep pot of water
• Dash white wine vinegar (optional)

METHOD
Bring pot to a boil, then turn down to a gentle simmer.
Add eggs once simmering — starting from boiling gives enough stored heat to cook multiple eggs.
Use a deep pan so eggs set before reaching the bottom (better shape).
Crack eggs into a cup.
Lower each egg into the natural simmer spots in the water.
Let them sit and set — do not boil or aggressively move.
After ~2½ mins, lift one with a spoon and gently jiggle.
Ripple / wobble = whites not fully set. Solid, bouncy jiggle = ready. Cook longer for firmer / well-done yolk.

NOTES
Fresh eggs hold tighter and shape cleaner. Simmer only — boiling breaks the whites. Vinegar helps the whites set faster (optional).
