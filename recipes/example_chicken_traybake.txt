Chicken Traybake

BASE
• 4 chicken thighs (skin on)
• 2 tbsp olive oil
• 500g baby potatoes, halved
• 2 red peppers, sliced
• 1 onion, sliced
• 4 garlic cloves
• 1 tsp smoked paprika
• Salt and pepper

METHOD
1. Preheat oven to 200°C.
2. Toss potatoes, peppers, onion and garlic in oil and paprika.
3. Add chicken, season, and roast for 45 minutes until golden.

TOTAL TIME
55 minutes

NOTES
Great with crusty bread.
