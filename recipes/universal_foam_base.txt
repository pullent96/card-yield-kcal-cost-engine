Universal Foam Base — 1L Liquid (Chef Version)

BASE
• 1000 ml liquid (juice, stock, milk, cream, purée, infusion)

FOAM AGENTS (choose texture / stability):
LECITHIN — light air / surface foam
• 3–6 g soy lecithin

GELATINE — stable warm foam / espumas
• 12–16 g soft foam
• 16–20 g firmer hold

XANTHAN — stabilised foam / longer hold
• 1–2 g

ULTRATEX — thickened foam base
• 15–25 g

GELLASPESSA — creamy stable foam
• 10–20 g

CREAM (natural foam base)
• 200–400 ml cream added to liquid for whippable foam

METHOD
LECITHIN: Blend into liquid → use hand blender at surface → creates instant light foam. Best served immediately; not long stable.
GELATINE: Bloom → dissolve into warm liquid → load into siphon → charge. Sets slightly as it cools, giving a more stable espuma.
XANTHAN: Blend into liquid → rest 5 mins → charge in siphon or whisk foam. Helps hold structure longer.
ULTRATEX: Blend into liquid to lightly thicken → charge or whisk. Thicker base = more stable foam.
GELLASPESSA: Blend into liquid → hydrate 2–3 mins → charge. Creates smoother, creamier foam texture.
CREAM: Add to liquid → whip or siphon. Natural stable foam with rich mouthfeel.

NOTES
Reference recipe — choose foam agent based on desired stability and texture. All agents work with the same 1L liquid base.
