Bolognese

BASE
500 g minced beef (or pork/beef)
200 g onion
100 g carrot
100 g celery
2 garlic cloves
400 g tomatoes
100 ml red wine
200 ml stock
30 ml tomato paste
Salt and pepper

METHOD
Sweat soffritto slowly.
Brown mince separately.
Combine.
Add garlic.
Deglaze with wine.
Add tomato, stock and paste.
Simmer 1–2 hours.
