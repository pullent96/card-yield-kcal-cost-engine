Beef / Veal Stock (Jus / Demi / Gravy Base)

BASE
Collagen-rich bones (veal knuckles, chicken feet, pig trotters, marrow)
2 onions
2 carrots
2 celery sticks
2–3 garlic cloves
30 ml tomato purée
200–250 ml red wine
15 ml oil or clarified butter
2–3 L cold water
Herbs (thyme, bay, parsley stalks)
Salt and pepper

METHOD
Roast bones at 200°C for 20–40 mins.
Sweat vegetables in fat.
Add tomato purée.
Deglaze with wine.
Add bones and water.
Simmer 3–4 hours, skim.
Strain.
Reduce to stock, jus or demi.
Season last.
Chill and skim fat.
