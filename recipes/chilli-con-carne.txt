TITLE: Chilli Con Carne

BASE:
- 500g beef mince
- 2 onions, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 red pepper, diced
- 400g tin chopped tomatoes
- 400g tin kidney beans, drained
- 2 tbsp tomato puree
- 1 tbsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste

METHOD:
1. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
2. Add the onions and cook for 5 minutes until softened.
3. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
4. Add the beef mince and brown for 8 minutes, breaking up with a spoon.
5. Stir in the chilli powder, cumin and smoked paprika.
6. Add the red pepper, chopped tomatoes and tomato puree. Stir well.
7. Drain and add the kidney beans.
8. Season with salt and pepper. Reduce heat to low.
9. Simmer uncovered for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
10. Serve with rice, jacket potato, or tortilla chips.

NOTES:
- Can be frozen for up to 3 months.
- Tastes even better the next day.
- Add dark chocolate (20g) for a richer flavour.
